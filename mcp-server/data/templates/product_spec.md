# Product Specification (Epic)

Standard template for a Product Specification / Epic that bridges the gap between high-level vision and granular user stories.

**Quality bar:** A reader can understand the "why", who it is for, what is strictly in MVP, and the primary workflows, without needing to ping the author. No sprint-level tasks or UI pixel details.

## Background & Value Proposition
- What problem are we solving?
- Why is it important to solve now?
- How do we measure success?

## Target Audience
- Who are the primary users/personas?
- What are their core pain points?

## Scope & Phasing
- **In Scope (MVP):** What MUST be delivered to validate the hypothesis?
- **Future Scope (v2+):** What are we explicitly deferring?
- **Out of Scope / Non-Goals:** What will we never do?

## Core Workflows
Describe the 2-3 primary user journeys (e.g., "User authenticates and views dashboard").
Keep it narrative; leave Given/When/Then to the user stories.

   - Step 2...
2. **[Workflow 2 Name]**
   - Step 1...
   - Step 2...

## Risks & Dependencies
- What teams, APIs, or existing systems do we depend on?
- What are the biggest technical or product risks?
